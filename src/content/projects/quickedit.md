Open a file, change what you need and close it. QuickEdit saves files byte for byte as they came in, except what you
changed.

- **Files as tabs** and **folders** with a file tree, Quick Open and file icons.
- **Syntax highlighting** with tree-sitter for dozens of languages, with code folding.
- **Safe edits.** Encodings, line endings and indentation are kept, and invisible characters are always marked.
- **Markdown and HTML preview**, live from unsaved edits.
- **CSV and TSV table mode**, and big files that stay fast.
