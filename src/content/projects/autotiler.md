Turn a small terrain template into a complete autotile tileset: the 47-tile blob set or a 16-tile dual grid, ready for
Godot 4, Tiled or any engine.

Use the [web version](https://itsjavi.com/autotiler/) with nothing to install, or get the
[desktop apps](https://github.com/itsjavi/autotiler/releases/latest) for macOS, Windows and Linux. Also on
[itch.io](https://route1rodent.itch.io/autotiler).
