Lamina has the layout you know from Photoshop and Affinity: tools on the left, the options bar above the canvas and
panels on the right, with commands under the names and shortcuts you'd look for.

- **Layers** and groups with blend modes, layer masks, clipping masks and layer styles.
- **Adjustment layers** edited live: Levels, Curves, Exposure, Hue/Saturation, Color Balance and more.
- **Free Transform**, distort, snapping, align and distribute.
- **Selections** including Object Selection, Select Subject, Color Range and Content-Aware Fill.
- **Works with AI agents** through the `lamina` command-line tool or as an MCP server.

Signed and notarized by Apple, and it updates itself.
